Privacy Policy — Reeling Loop

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Reeling Loop is a video saver app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing about you.

Information we collect

None. Reeling Loop has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store or share your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information.

How the app works

When you paste a video link and tap to save, the app fetches that video directly from the public page you linked and stores the file in your device's Download folder. The video links you paste and the videos you save never leave your device. The app does not upload anything to any server.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to fetch the video you asked to save, and storage access (on older Android versions) only to write the saved file to your Download folder. Both are used solely for the feature you requested.

Children

Reeling Loop is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
